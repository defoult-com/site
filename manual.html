Manual

An 8-part performance step sequencer with a built-in drum machine, MIDI output, pattern banks, step locks, live looping and audio bounce. Everything is playable by touch, mouse or keyboard.

1 · Layout

  • Top bar — transport (play / stop / record / settings), tempo & metronome, and the four mode tabs: PTRN STEP KEYB CTRL.
  • Parts row — 8 parts. Tap to select the active part; the grid, display and CTRL panel always show the active part.
  • Hold buttons — MUTE / SOLO: hold and tap parts to mute or solo them. ALIGN re-syncs parts to the bar. SELECT is a modifier used in the PTRN tab.
  • Step grid — 16 pads per page, up to 64 steps. Its meaning changes per tab (steps, pattern slots, keyboard).
  • Virtual display — shows the playing Bank · Pattern and a grid of value cells. Adjust any cell by dragging or the scroll wheel; double-click / double-tap resets it. Cells can also be driven by incoming MIDI CC.
  • Action buttons — the four buttons above the grid change function per tab (described in each section).
  • Status bar — every action is confirmed here. Read it when unsure.

2 · Transport, Tempo & Recording Arm

  • Play / Stop (Space) starts and stops the sequencer. Queued events (pattern changes) land on bar downbeats.
  • REC arms live recording: notes played in KEYB mode and knob moves in CTRL mode are written into the running sequence as steps and step locks.
  • Tempo — drag the tempo readout (or scroll) to change BPM. The metronome button toggles a click with a bar accent.

3 · STEP tab

  • Tap a pad to place or remove a step. Q–I and A–K mirror the 16 pads.
  • Hold a pad — the display switches to that step's own page (STEP n — PART n): NOTE · ACCENT · GATE · PROB · OCT · VELOCITY · TIME OFST · RETRIG. Drag the cells to shape just that step (probability, micro-timing, ratchets…). A removed step remembers its settings and restores them when re-placed.
  • A dot on a pad means the step carries step locks (see CTRL). A red dot means those locks are currently muted.

On the display — SEQUENCER PART

The display shows SEQUENCER PART N: eight cells shaping the active part’s timing. All of them are draggable / scrollable here:

  • SEQ STRT / SEQ END — the part’s loop window (1–64 steps; also draggable on the range bar). They dim when step-skips override them.
  • SEQ DIR — playback direction. SPEED — per-part speed multiplier (polyrhythms).
  • OFFSET — rotates the sequence; the grid shows the shifted result. SWING — off-beat shuffle.
  • MIDI DLY — note-echo amount; hold MUTE to reach DLY TIME (echo division) on the same cell.
  • DLY FB — echo feedback; hold MUTE to reach PITCH FB (±12 st per echo — pitched delays).

The MUTE-shifted alternates apply to drag, wheel and incoming CC alike. Holding a step pad switches the display to that step’s own parameters; holding a part button shows the part’s note defaults.

4 · PTRN tab — banks, patterns & looper

  • 8 banks × 64 patterns. The parts row becomes the bank picker; the grid shows that bank's pattern slots (lit = has data).
  • Tap a slot — stopped: loads immediately. Playing: queues the change for the next bar downbeat (slot blinks, status confirms). The display always shows what is actually playing, while you browse freely.
  • Hold SELECT and tap a slot to select it without loading.
  • Virtual display — VELOCITY MIXER: eight faders scaling each part's velocity (0–100). With the drum machine active each fader is labeled with that part's instrument. Drag them for quick live mixing, or drive them with incoming CC.
  • LOOP 1/4 · 1/8 · 1/16 · 1/32 — hold an action button to loop the playing material at that length; release to resume exactly where playback would have been. Loops can be layered.
  • A pattern change made while looping waits in the background — the loop is untouched; on release the new pattern enters on the next bar.

On the display — VELOCITY MIXER

  • The display becomes a velocity mixer: one cell per part (0–100%) scaling that part’s output level/velocity — a quick performance mixer while switching patterns. Drag, scroll or drive it with incoming CC.
  • The header always shows the Bank · Pattern that is playing, independent of what you are browsing.

5 · KEYB tab

  • The grid becomes a playable keyboard for the active part; the range bar sets the octave.
  • Virtual display — KEYBOARD: VELOCITY · SCALE · ROOT · OCTAVE · ARP · RATE · RANGE · GATE. Scale and root lock the keys to a scale; the ARP cells configure the arpeggiator (mode, rate, octave range, gate). Each cell has its own assignable input-CC number.
  • ROLL 1/4 · 1/8 · 1/16 · 1/32 — hold an action button while holding notes to repeat them at that division.
  • With REC armed and the transport running, played notes are written into the sequence at the current position.

On the display — key & arp settings

  • VELOCITY — how hard played notes hit. SCALE and ROOT — reshape the keyboard to a scale so every pad is in key. OCTAVE — base octave.
  • ARP, RATE, RANGE — the arpeggiator: mode, speed division and octave span; hold notes and it plays them (RATE/RANGE dim while ARP is off).
  • Every cell here carries an editable CC number, so an external controller can play the whole panel.

6 · CTRL tab — parameters & locks

  • On the display: SOUND CTRL — 8 parameter cells for the active part. With the internal drum machine they are its sound parameters (the set depends on the sound type); in MIDI mode they are 8 assignable CCs (drag the CC number to reassign) sent to the part's output.
  • Each cell has a global value — drag it any time; this is the value that plays wherever no lock exists.
  • Step lock: hold a step pad — the display retitles to LOCK — STEP N and the cells now show that step's values — then drag a cell: that value plays only on that step; the next unlocked step snaps back to the global value. Locked cells are highlighted; double-click a held cell to remove its lock.
  • With REC armed, turning cells while playing records locks as automation.

Sound types (drum machine)

  • Hold a part button (with the drum machine active) to open its PART SETUP panel: scrub TYPE through the nine engine voices and set VOICE to MONO or POLY.
  • The types: KICK · SNARE · TOM · RIM · CLAP · HIHAT (classic analog-style drum voices with pitch, decay and tone), FM (FM percussion/melodic voice), CHRD (a 3-oscillator chord voice with detune spread) and SAMPLE (load your own audio, with crush/rate reduction).
  • Each type brings its own parameter set to the CTRL cells — so what you lock per step depends on the sound type.

FX hold panels

  • Hold MUTE in the CTRL tab → the display switches to the master DIST + EQ (distortion on/off and drive, EQ off / pre-drive / post-drive with its bands).
  • Hold SOLO → the send REVERB + DELAY parameters.
  • These hold panels behave like every other cell: drag, scroll-wheel, double-click to reset — and incoming MIDI CC edits them too while the panel is held open.

7 · Sound engine & FX

  • In Settings, DRUM MACHINE switches the parts between the internal sound engine and external MIDI.
  • Each internal part has a sound editor (voice parameters, sample loading, pan, effect sends). Its type and mono/poly voice are set in the PART SETUP panel (hold a part button in CTRL).
  • Master FX — drive/distortion with EQ, a send reverb (decay, damping, algorithm) and a tempo-synced delay. All of them are hands-on from the CTRL tab via the MUTE / SOLO hold panels. Combined with PITCH FB, delays can spiral up or down in pitch.

8 · MIDI

  • Per part: output port and channel (Settings). Notes, echoes and CTRL CCs are sent per part.
  • Incoming CC is mapped to whatever the display currently shows — sequencer cells in STEP (including the MUTE-shifted alternates), CTRL cells in CTRL, and the velocity mixer in PTRN.

9 · Audio bounce

  • The recorder bar captures the master output. BOUNCE while playing arms and starts on the next bar; pressing STOP lets the bar finish first — takes are exact whole bars, ready to loop. Press stop twice to cut immediately.
  • From a stopped transport, BOUNCE starts playback and captures from the very first step.
  • SAVE WAV exports the take; the preview button auditions it first.

10 · Projects & saving

  • Settings row: FILE SAVE exports the whole project (all banks, patterns, sounds, settings) as a file; FILE LOAD imports one; NEW resets everything.
  • AUTO-SAVE keeps the project persisted in the browser and commits pattern edits back to their slot automatically.
